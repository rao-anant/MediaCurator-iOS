## Description (4000 max)
GalleryCurator helps you take back control of a photo library that's grown out of hand — calmly, one month at a time, and completely on your device.

Most photo apps just show you everything at once. GalleryCurator gives you a simple system for actually getting through it:

• Browse by month — Your photos and videos are grouped by year and month, newest first, so you always know where you are.

• Mark months "done" — Finished reviewing March? Hide it. It disappears from your working view (your photos are never deleted) so you can see, at a glance, how much you have left to curate. Your progress is remembered — even if you reinstall the app.

• Find duplicates — GalleryCurator detects exact duplicate photos and shows them side by side, so you can keep one and free up space with confidence.

• Browse by place — See your library organized by city and country, and revisit everywhere you've been.

• Delete with a safety net — Swipe to remove photos you don't want. They go to your system Recently Deleted, and a quick Undo is always there if you change your mind.

• Search — Find photos fast by file name.

Private by design:
GalleryCurator does its work entirely on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account to create, no sign-in, nothing uploaded to a server, and no ads or tracking. It reads your photo library only to show it to you, and only with your permission.

GalleryCurator is for anyone staring at "10,000 photos" and not knowing where to start. Do a month. Mark it done. Watch the pile shrink.
